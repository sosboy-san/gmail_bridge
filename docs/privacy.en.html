Privacy policy

This policy covers the owner's personal deployment associated with this site. People running the public code separately are responsible for their own data management and explanations.

Information and purpose

To import the owner's mail, the application processes message bodies, senders, recipients, subjects, attachments and read state. Authorized Google OAuth access is used for Gmail import, labels/read-state updates and checking import state. Rejected attachments may be saved to Drive.

Storage and transmission

Mail and attachments are stored in the authenticated Google account's Gmail/Drive. Tokens, configuration, processing records, logs and backups stay on the operator's PC/NAS. The application does not make Drive sharing public.

When ntfy is enabled, new-mail and outage/recovery events are sent to the configured server. New-mail notifications optionally include sender name/address, subject and body preview. Display is off by default, but metadata and previews are saved locally at import time even when display is off. Protect the database and backups as mail data. Third-party services apply their own terms and policies.

Use limitations

The owner's deployment does not use retrieved information for advertising, sale or AI-model training. Use and transfer of Google API information comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use.

Retention and deletion

The operator manages Gmail messages and Drive files in those services, and local records/logs/backups according to retention and operational choices. Stopping the app or revoking access does not delete saved data. Optional source cleanup deletes imported mail only after the delay and safety checks.

Revoke access

Revoke authorization in Google Account connections. Stop the app and remove local credentials to stop continued access. Delete retained data/backups separately as needed.

Contact

For the personal OAuth app, use the support contact shown on its consent screen. For public code, use GitHub Issues. Never post credentials or private message contents.
